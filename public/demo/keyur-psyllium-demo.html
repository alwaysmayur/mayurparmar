About Keyur Industries

From a long-standing brand to a global psyllium supplier.

Keyur Industries describes itself as a manufacturer, exporter and marketer of psyllium seed, psyllium husk and psyllium husk powder since 1974. The company also has its Gulab Brand® Sat-Isabgol brand, established in 1948.

The company states that it supplies the international and domestic markets, including multinational and pharmaceutical customers, with different psyllium purity options.
